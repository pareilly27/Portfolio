About Me

Strip the fluff

I am Shreyanshi Satodia, a Graphic Designer based in Dallas, Texas, with 7 years of experience turning bold ideas into impactful visuals through color, typography, layout, and thoughtful design. I believe design should be felt as much as it’s seen; it grabs attention, tells a story, and leaves an impression that lasts. My work blends creativity with strategy, weaving together visual language and purposeful communication across branding, editorial, packaging, and digital experiences.

Driven by curiosity, I’m always looking for fresh ways to connect people and ideas through design. And when I’m not creating, I’m usually chasing inspiration and greeting every dog I pass. Let’s create work that moves people.
