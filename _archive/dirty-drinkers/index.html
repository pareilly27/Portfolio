Dirty Drinkers

Dirty Drinkers is a bold, character-driven brand that taps into vintage cartoon nostalgia with a gritty, modern twist. Featuring thick black outlines, expressive hand-drawn faces, and a punchy red-and-white palette, the design embraces a loud, unapologetic attitude. The custom typography and sticker-like illustrations create a collectible, rebellious feel. With its comic-book energy and nostalgic humor, Dirty Drinkers offers a playful, in-your-face soda experience unlike anything else on the shelf.
