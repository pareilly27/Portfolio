Ignite

Ignite Home Features specializes in high-end fireplaces, delivering premium heating solutions for both residential and commercial spaces. To match their vision, the new brand identity evokes sophistication through elegant typography, a sleek, muted palette, and upscale visuals. The design conveys warmth, style, and quality mirroring the luxury of their bespoke fireplace installations. From printed materials to a meticulously crafted brand book, every element reinforces Ignite's position as a premier provider of refined fireplace features.
