What If

What If is a range of refreshing herb-based coolers inspired by nature's calming properties. The packaging design focuses on evoking a sense of freshness and tranquility through vibrant colors and custom illustrations that highlight the natural ingredients. Each element from the soothing palette to the organic visual details—was carefully chosen to reflect the purity and serenity at the heart of the product. The result is a visually uplifting brand that feels as refreshing as the drink itself.
