The long version

I am Shreyanshi Satodia, a creative designer and strategist with seven years
of experience creating brands and digital experiences with award-winning
work across hospitality, SaaS, entertainment, nonprofit, and F&B. I enjoy
taking projects from concept to launch, turning ideas into systems that feel
thoughtful, cohesive, and built to last.

I have learned that the first idea is rarely the best one. It's just polite enough
to introduce the rest. That's why I like the messy middle, the stage where
questions outnumber answers and everything still has room to change. It's
also where the strongest ideas usually show up.

I have a habit of rearranging things until they make sense. Design turned out
to be a pretty good place to channel that. Whether I am building an identity,
designing a website, or developing a campaign, I am always looking for the
one idea that ties everything together. The fun part is figuring it out. The
design just gets all the credit.
